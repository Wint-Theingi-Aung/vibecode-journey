# The Beginning

## From Being Out of Touch to Reconnecting with the Tech World

In my current role, I work mainly as a SysAdmin rather than focusing on software development and application deployment. I'm always busy with various tasks and responsibilities, so I haven't had much time to explore what's happening in the tech world. However, I had occasionally experimented with AI-assisted coding and tried building a few things with AI.

When I registered for the **Vibe Code Tour**, I honestly wasn't sure if I would be selected. The mentor initially announced that he would accept only 10 participants. However, as he mentioned later, more than 300 people applied through social media. Eventually, he selected around 200 participants and decided to share his knowledge through lectures and provide free API tokens to support our learning journey.

After being selected, we were invited to join Telegram and Discord channels to connect with the mentor and other participants. I already had a Discord account, but I had rarely used it before. So, this was also a chance for me to start using Discord more actively and connect with people in the tech community.

As the journey began, I had the opportunity to meet people from different technical backgrounds and professional fields. The mentor divided us into 20 teams, and I was assigned to **Team 7**, which consisted of 10 members. Fortunately, I found myself surrounded by friendly and supportive teammates, making the experience even more enjoyable.

Perhaps, this journey will be the beginning of my return to the tech world — a chance to reconnect, learn new things, explore new technologies, and rediscover my passion for building software.
